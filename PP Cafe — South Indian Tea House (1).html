Our story

Rooted in Kerala's tea-shop culture, poured for today.

PP Cafe began as a single brass kettle and a wooden bench by the road. Today the same slow-brewed chai, the same hand-pulled froth, meets a room built for lingering evenings — brass fittings, warm light, and the smell of cardamom drifting from the kitchen.
